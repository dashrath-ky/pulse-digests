Finshots · Wed Aug 19
The RBI wants to stop NBFCs from offering revolving credit, the credit-card-like lines you can borrow and repay over and over. Its worry is evergreening: a stressed borrower can quietly draw from the unused limit to repay the old loan and still look healthy on paper. Banks get to keep offering it because they can see the borrower's salary and account activity, while NBFCs only see the credit line. Bajaj Finance, Tata Capital and Jio Financial all fell on the news.

A deep dive into Inkling, the first model Thinking Machines trained from scratch, released under Apache 2.0 on Hugging Face. It's 975 billion parameters on paper but only about 41 billion run for any given token, because each of its 66 layers picks 6 experts out of 256. Most layers only see a short window of recent text while a few see everything, which is how it gets to a million-token context. Mira Murati's company also went back to an older position-encoding method most labs abandoned years ago.
