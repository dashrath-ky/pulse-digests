Finshots · Wed Sep 23
People keep saying India is "privatising" ISRO, but that's not quite what's happening. Under the 2023 space policy, ISRO is supposed to stick to research, deep-space missions and human spaceflight, and hand mature tech over to private companies to build and sell. You can already see it with rockets: HAL and L&T now manufacture PSLVs, and HAL got the SSLV design outright. The budget tells the same story, with money for running the INSAT satellite fleet dropping from ₹345 crore to ₹131 crore over five years.

Morning Brew · Tue Sep 22
The $110 billion Paramount and Warner Bros. merger is back on after California and 11 other states agreed to drop their antitrust lawsuit. In return, Paramount has to keep its headquarters and studio lots in California for five years, release at least 30 movies a year in theaters, and let a board of journalists protect CNN and CBS News from interference. It didn't have to sell off anything. The combined company will carry up to $87 billion in debt, and analysts expect job cuts as it chases $6 billion in savings.
