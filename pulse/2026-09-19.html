Finshots · Sat Sep 19
India finished its 2,800 km Dedicated Freight Corridor, a rail network built only for goods trains. One train recently moved as much cargo from JNPT port in Maharashtra to Vadodara as 250 trucks would have. The catch is the last mile, since goods still have to reach the tracks and leave them by road, which is why trucks still dominate Indian freight.
