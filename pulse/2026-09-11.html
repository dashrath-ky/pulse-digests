The Aditya Birla Group is spending Rs 1,800 crore to get into electrical wires and cables with a new brand called Ultravolt, which starts life as India's second-largest wire producer by capacity. It has real advantages: Hindalco can supply it copper, and UltraTech's 5,000 cement outlets give it a foot in the door. But cement dealers aren't electrical dealers, and Polycab still owns roughly 30% of the organised market with Rs 4,194 crore of net cash to fight back. The likelier outcome isn't Polycab losing its crown, it's everyone's margins getting squeezed as new capacity floods in.
