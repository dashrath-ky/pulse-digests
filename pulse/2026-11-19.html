GPUs & Capacity
Nvidia spelled out when its data center loan guarantees actually apply. Interim CEO Colette Kress said a guarantee only kicks in once the customer has its own financing and gets the facility built with Nvidia gear installed. If the project slips or fails, the guarantee goes away. Nvidia says it will keep financing data centers but be "far more selective," and TechCrunch cites reports that OpenAI's Ohio backstop was cut from $250 billion to $105 billion. Neoclouds that leaned on Nvidia's balance sheet to add capacity now carry more of the risk themselves, which could slow how fast new GPU supply comes online.

Ray & Training
Ray 2.58.2 is out, and it's a security patch. The keys of the eMudhra intermediate certificate authority were reported compromised in October, and pip trusts its own bundled certificates on top of the system ones, so updating the OS alone doesn't protect it. This release moves every CA bundle Ray installs or ships to the fixed versions, same as 2.59.1. Worth checking which Ray versions our images pin.

Inference Serving
Hugging Face ran a 180-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model on a Mac with 32GB of memory. The trick is to keep the "experts" (the parts of the model only used for some tokens) on the SSD and stream them into a small cache as needed. It hits about 14 tokens per second, close to a dedicated engine. It's a new `experts_offload` option in Transformers that's still an open pull request, not a release.
