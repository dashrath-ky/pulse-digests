A Dutch company called Fairphone just brought its repairable phone to the US, and it is a direct shot at how glued-shut modern phones have become. It ships with a screwdriver, the parts unscrew, and a replacement battery costs about a third of what Apple charges. Software support runs to 2033, which matters when the world already throws away 62 million tonnes of electronics a year.

Prompt injection has no single fix, so the advice is to stack defences. At the model level you tag untrusted text as data and train the model to rank the system prompt above everything else. At the system level you cut the agent's tools to the minimum, require a human to approve anything sensitive, and split planning from execution so the model reading untrusted content has no tools to abuse.
