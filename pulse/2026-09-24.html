India's company law tribunal (the NCLT, which handles corporate bankruptcy cases) is badly short of people. The law says these cases should wrap up in 330 days, but delays now stretch as long as 738 days. The tribunal should have 63 members and has 50, with 18 more retiring by the end of next year, so 18 benches are only running half days. The Supreme Court has blamed the government for sitting on appointments, and a new law passed last month sets up an independent commission to run tribunals.

TLDR IT · Wed Sep 23
A security writeup explains why company AI copilots keep leaking data. The trouble starts when one assistant can read private data, reads content from outsiders (like an incoming email), and can also send things out. With all three, a single crafted email can trick it into handing data to an attacker, and this has already happened to Microsoft 365 Copilot and Salesforce Agentforce. The fix is to give assistants fewer permissions and never all three at once.

A clear explainer on how to customize an AI model. Start with better prompts. If the model is missing information, feed it the right documents at question time (called RAG).
