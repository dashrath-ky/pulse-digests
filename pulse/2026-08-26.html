ByteByteGo · Tue Aug 25
Researchers found a way to read an AI model's hidden thoughts. Providers like OpenAI, Anthropic and Google hand you an encrypted blob of the model's full reasoning so they don't have to store it themselves. A team at MATS, ELLIS Tübingen and Max Planck showed you can replay that blob into a cheaper model in the same family and it will happily print the reasoning in plain text. That matters because the reasoning trace often holds the raw stuff, including credentials the model read while doing your task.
