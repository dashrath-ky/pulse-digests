Finshots · Wed Sep 30
The UN General Assembly adopted a draft of its first Declaration on Sea Level Rise, which treats rising seas as a threat to whole countries. Places like the Maldives (just 1.5 metres above sea level) and Tuvalu could lose much of their land by 2050. The big open question is what happens to a country's statehood, citizenship and fishing rights if its islands go underwater, because current international law has no real rulebook for that. Closer to home, Mumbai and Kolkata are among the cities where more than 14 million people are at risk.
