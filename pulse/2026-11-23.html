Models & Research
OpenAI's chief scientist, Jakub Pachocki, wants the AI labs to agree on a shared speedometer. His argument is that today's models are mostly very good text predictors, so how well they predict text (a score called perplexity, where lower is better) tracks how capable they get. OpenAI published its own numbers to start that conversation: GPT-4 sat around 0.91, and a model near 0.76 started solving open math problems. He proposes "common limits" on this score that no lab crosses until better safety tools exist, and he expects pretraining progress could speed up as soon as 2027. Sam Altman called it the most important thing OpenAI has published in a while. (also in TechCrunch)

TechCrunch got three of Anthropic's biggest early backers to explain why they bet billions before the IPO. Notable Capital bought in at a $61.5B valuation after finding developers had switched to Claude for most of their work. Singapore's GIC put in over $4.5B across three rounds, and Baillie Gifford put in $1.5B, pointing to steady execution and strong margins. The IPO raised $81.3B, the largest ever, at roughly $1.5T, so those early stakes are up about 15x on paper.

Reliance shut the last of its 7-Eleven stores in India in September. The chain lost about ₹90 crore on ₹91 crore of revenue in FY26. Quick commerce and neighbourhood kiranas already covered the convenience it was selling, and Indians eat out far less often than people in Japan. A comeback would need a different playbook, such as stores that double as delivery hubs.
