Finshots · Mon Aug 24
India's biggest stock exchange, the NSE, is finally close to going public after a decade of regulatory trouble, and it has run into a funny problem. The rules say an exchange can't list its own shares on its own platform, so the NSE has to list on its rival, the BSE. It plans to use an existing "permitted to trade" route so its stock can still be bought and sold on the NSE too, which would also make it eligible for the Nifty 50 one day. The odd part: the Nifty is run by a company the NSE owns.
Morning Brew · Sun Aug 23
Morning Brew ran a whole issue on "sweaty startups", the trend of people skipping the AI app idea and starting boring physical businesses instead. Think power washing, mold removal, storage units, car detailing. The numbers back it up: the share of top 1% income coming from midsize regional businesses like plumbing and flooring rose to 34.9% in 2022 from 30.3% in 2014, and Gen Z business applications jumped 66% in the year to June.
Morning Brew · Sun Aug 23
Car detailing has quietly turned into a real career path, partly because scrub-down videos rack up millions of views on TikTok. One former Amazon warehouse worker now pulls in around $18,500 a month washing cars, plus another $50,000 from teaching other people how to do it.
Lenny's Newsletter · Sun Aug 23
Jen Abel walked through the full enterprise sales cycle step by step on Lenny's podcast. Her argument is that the familiar five-stage CRM pipeline is really a forecasting tool for managers, not a process a seller can follow, and the actual cycle is closer to 15 steps. She covers getting that first meeting, running an intro call that pulls out information before you demo anything, and structuring a short pilot with success criteria agreed upfront.
