ByteByteGo · Wed Sep 02
A good explainer on why RAG chatbots confidently give wrong answers. The culprit is usually the embedding model, the piece that turns text into numbers so the system can search by meaning rather than keywords. If it retrieves the wrong passage, no amount of upgrading the language model will save you, and swapping the embedding model later is expensive because everything has to be re-indexed.
