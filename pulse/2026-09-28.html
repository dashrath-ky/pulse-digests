Finshots · Mon Sep 28
India's insurance regulator, IRDAI, wants to change how insurance gets sold. A new draft proposal would cap the commissions insurers pay to agents, banks and online brokers, and stop banks from forcing you to buy a policy along with a loan. The push comes from mis-selling: complaints rose 14% to over 26,600 in FY25. Shares of distributors like Policybazaar have dropped as much as 30% since. (Finshots owns Ditto, one of those distributors, so they're openly affected.)
Morning Brew · Sun Sep 27
Young, wealthy investors are moving money into "alternatives" like private company shares, real estate, crypto and even Pokémon cards. About 20% of millennial portfolios sit in these assets, compared with 6% for boomers, per Goldman Sachs. Banks are eager to sell them. The catch is that many lock up your money and tell you far less than a stock does, so losses can be bigger and harder to see coming.
Morning Brew · Sun Sep 27
Two in five Gen Z Americans say sports betting counts as investing, according to a Bank of America survey. Half of Gen Z in a separate Betterment survey said they'd moved money meant for investing into bets instead. The math is against them. Sportsbooks bake a fee into the odds, so you lose over time even if you win half your bets, and Gen Z gets back about 80 cents per dollar wagered.
Lenny's Newsletter · Sun Sep 27
Leadership coach Molly Graham (ex-Google, Facebook) says tech is going through a wave of grief, loneliness and burnout right now. Her famous "give away your Legos" advice, which means handing off your work as the company grows, doesn't work the same way when the one taking it is AI. She also thinks the fear about AI wiping out jobs is overblown.
