Models & Research
Two mathematicians claimed proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis within hours of each other. Anthropic's Levent Alpöge says he proved it, and the more general version, working with Claude, and released 1.3GB of Lean code (machine-checked proof) instead of a paper. Elliot Glazer confirmed the formal statement is right and puts the odds of a Lean bug under 1%. The second claim, from Alessandro Masullo, is fully human and still a 940-page draft. Latent Space's line sums it up: the AI proof is "probably correct but incomprehensible," the human one "understandable" but likely flawed. (also in TechCrunch, The Verge, Simon Willison)
