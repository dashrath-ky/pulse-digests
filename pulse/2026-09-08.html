Finshots · Tue Sep 08
Indian drink makers have been short on aluminium cans since April and are scrambling to switch to glass and PET bottles before the festive season. China cut aluminium exports in late 2024, then the West Asia crisis shut Gulf smelters and blocked shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, knocking out about 9% of global smelting capacity. Prices peaked near $3,800 a tonne and are still about 25% above last year's average. India makes plenty of raw aluminium but not the thin food-grade sheet cans need, and a new quality order on imported sheet made the shortage worse.

ByteByteGo · Mon Sep 07
A deep dive on error handling in LLM apps. The core point is that a successful API response does not mean success, since the model can return broken JSON, hallucinate, hit its token limit or call the wrong tool. The piece covers classifying failures, retrying properly, timeouts, falling back to smaller models, circuit breakers, rate limiting and making tool calls idempotent.
Lenny's Newsletter · Mon Sep 07
On How I AI, Claire Vo explains why she moved her entire agent stack from OpenClaw to Grok Bot, mostly because OpenClaw became too hard to keep running. She now runs bots for six inboxes, PR reviews, SOC 2 fixes and customer refunds. Her takeaways: treat each bot like a new hire with a name and a scope, put high-stakes actions like refunds behind an approval button, and give the agent a voice profile because Grok writes too tersely by default.
