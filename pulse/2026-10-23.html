eMudhra, an Indian company that issues the digital certificates behind the padlock in your browser, shipped some certificates with keys so weak a computer could guess them in a fraction of a second. The stock fell to a 52-week low and lost close to ₹1,000 crore in value after the government ordered a security probe.
