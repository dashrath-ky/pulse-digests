Gen Z has a new name for job hopping: "lily padding", jumping to whichever role pays best right now. The math backs them up. More than half of Indian professionals got an appraisal under 10% this year, while switching jobs typically lands a 20-35% hike. A Foundit survey found 96% of professionals are open to moving, and some firms now pay an extra 10-15% just for candidates who can join in 30 days instead of 90.

American shoppers are pulling back. Retail sales fell 0.6% in July, the first drop in nine months and the steepest since May 2025. Part of it is Amazon moving Prime Day to June, which pulled spending forward, but the rest looks like genuine weakness: consumer sentiment down about 8% this month, 23,000 jobs lost, and inflation still around 3.4% against the Fed's 2% target. Kevin Warsh and the Fed will probably sit on rates in September.
