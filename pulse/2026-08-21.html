Finshots · Fri Aug 21
India's market regulator SEBI accused two firms of rigging the SENSEX's closing price and pocketing about ₹3.68 crore. The trick was possible because SEBI recently replaced the old closing-price method (an average of the last 30 minutes of trading) with a 20-minute auction at the end of the day. In that short window, a JPMorgan-linked fund called Copthall placed ₹66 crore of buy orders in the first two seconds, making up 99.9% of all buying and shoving the index up 362 points, then cancelled them. A broker named Mansi did the reverse with ₹145 crore of sell orders to push the index down 233 points. Both had options bets that paid off from the move.

TLDR · Thu Aug 20
Moderna and Merck say their personalised cancer vaccine worked in a late-stage melanoma trial. The vaccine is built per patient: doctors compare the genes in someone's tumour to their healthy cells, pick out up to 34 mutations unique to the cancer, and encode those into an mRNA shot given alongside Merck's Keytruda. Patients went longer before the cancer came back, and it spread less often. Moderna's stock jumped 177% on the news, Merck's 13%. The data hasn't been independently reviewed yet.
