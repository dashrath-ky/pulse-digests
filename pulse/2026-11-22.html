GPUs & Capacity
Nvidia is giving off two very different signals. On paper the business is booming: last quarter's revenue was $115.8 billion, double a year ago, and it expects about $130 billion next quarter. Interim CEO Colette Kress says Nvidia has locked in memory and packaging capacity and is working with TSMC to close the gap between supply and demand. Meanwhile the stock fell 14% on Friday after federal regulators said they're looking into its accounting, its seventh weekly loss in a row, and Elon Musk and a petition are pushing for Jensen Huang to come back. For GPU capacity planning, the supply story looks steady for now. The turmoil is about leadership and investor trust, not chips running short. (also in Morning Brew)

Open Source & Tools
Graham Dumpleton, who wrote mod_wsgi 20 years ago, released Muru, a Rust server for running Python web apps. It handles TLS and static files itself so you don't need a web server in front, and it does blue/green releases, canaries and instant rollback. It even has an MCP server so AI agents can deploy and roll back. It's alpha, and built with heavy help from coding agents.
