ByteByteGo · Tue Sep 08
A deep dive into how American Express processes card payments on cloud infrastructure that can fail at any moment. The answer is cell-based architecture: each cell is a complete, self-contained copy of the payment stack with its own services and databases, and no cell makes blocking calls to another during a transaction. Cells divide the system by failure, not by function, so a bad cell can be pulled from rotation while the rest keep approving payments.
