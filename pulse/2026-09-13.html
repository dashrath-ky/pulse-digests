OpenAI says one of its AI systems cracked the Navier-Stokes problem, a 90-year-old maths puzzle about how fluids move that carries a $1 million prize. The company threw about 10,000 AI agents at it for 88 hours, burning roughly $15 million worth of compute, then had another model spend 17 hours writing the proof in a formal language a computer can check. The awkward part: two human mathematicians were already deep into the same approach, one of them using OpenAI's own Codex, and they say OpenAI sped up its effort right after hearing about their progress.
