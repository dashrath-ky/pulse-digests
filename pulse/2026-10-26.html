Finshots · Mon Oct 26
Elon Musk has publicly backed merging Tesla and SpaceX, which would create a company worth about $3.5 trillion. The logic is that the two already overlap a lot: SpaceX owns xAI, Tesla invested $2 billion in xAI, and they buy batteries and cars from each other. SpaceX is the cash machine here, with $4.4 billion in profit last quarter alone, while Tesla's profit fell 46% in 2025. The catch for Tesla shareholders is that they'd suddenly be funding rockets and AI data centers, and it would get much harder to tell which projects actually make money.
Morning Brew · Sun Oct 25
Halloween shopping keeps creeping earlier. Nearly half of Americans now start before October, and about one in four had started by early August this year. Retailers like it because it fills the slow stretch between July 4th and back-to-school, and it helps them avoid being stuck with unsold costumes on Nov 1. Americans are expected to spend $13.5 billion on Halloween this year.
