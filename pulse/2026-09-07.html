Finshots · Mon Sep 07
Sugar Cosmetics is now worth about 80% less than it was at its peak, and the numbers explain why. It raised roughly Rs 145 crore from A91 Partners last week at a valuation of Rs 550 to 600 crore, down from around Rs 2,700 crore in 2022. Revenue fell 20% to Rs 404 crore in FY25 while losses nearly doubled to Rs 135 crore.
Finshots · Mon Sep 07
The deeper problem is the math of Indian D2C beauty. Industry estimates put the cost of buying a new customer at around Rs 1,850, while the average order is only about Rs 890. So a brand spends roughly twice the first order just to land the customer, and lipstick isn't something people rebuy every month. Sugar also spread itself across skincare, teen products, and a mass-market line instead of dominating one category.

Family spending on kids' sports jumped 46% between 2019 and 2024. One Florida parent who paid $80 a season as a kid now spends $8,000 a year on her 12-year-old's club baseball.
