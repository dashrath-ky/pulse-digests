Finshots · Mon Sep 21
India's market regulator SEBI is running a pilot where corporate bonds live on a blockchain instead of in the usual pile of separate databases. REC, L&T and IIFL have already raised over ₹1,000 crore this way. The pitch is that interest payouts fire automatically via smart contracts, and the bond and the money change hands at the same instant, so a trade can't half-settle and leave you exposed if the other side blows up. Nothing changes for the buyer, the depository still holds the keys for you.

Gaming is now bigger than music and movies combined, over 3.5 billion players, but you own less of what you buy than ever. Sony goes digital-only for new PlayStation games from 2028 and GTA VI will be too. A proposed class action says Sony isn't upfront that you're buying a revocable licence, not a game.

Cloud gaming is the next squeeze. Take-Two's CEO expects a surge as AI demand pushes console prices up, but streaming turns gaming into a subscription, and Microsoft has already capped monthly Xbox Game Pass streaming hours because the cost scales with how much people play. Ads inside games are the likely release valve, Xbox started testing ad-supported streaming in July.
Morning Brew · Sun Sep 20
Hollywood finally cracked video game adaptations. The Super Mario Galaxy Movie is the fifth-biggest film of the year at over $1 billion worldwide, A Minecraft Movie did $960 million last year, and Fallout and The Last of Us proved it works on TV too. It runs the other way now as well, Paramount opened its own game studio in June to turn its films into games.
