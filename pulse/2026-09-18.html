Finshots · Fri Sep 18
Rentomojo rents out furniture and appliances on a monthly subscription, and it now holds more than half the market by subscriber count. Finshots digs into why it won: not marketing, but the boring operational stuff. It refurbished over 6 lakh items last year and put them back into circulation, so the same fridge earns money from several customers over its life. The catch is that it's an asset-heavy business, so depreciation of about ₹70 crore eats over 40% of its EBITDA, and it spent more on buying assets last year than it generated in operating cash.
