How does a private hospital actually make money? Mostly by squeezing more out of the beds it already has: filling them, treating more complex (and pricier) cases like cardiac and cancer care, and getting patients home sooner so the next one can come in. Fortis earns about ₹68,700 per occupied bed per day, and at both Fortis and Manipal, six high-end specialties bring in over 60% of revenue. The topic is timely because the Supreme Court just questioned hospital drug pricing, after hearing that a cancer drug sold to retailers for ₹2,700 carried an MRP of ₹27,000.
