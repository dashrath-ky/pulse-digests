Your biryani might one day arrive by drone. A US company called Matternet plans to start drone food deliveries in 2027 with Dave's Hot Chicken, and India's Skye Air has already done about 3.6 million drone drops, including 7-minute runs in Gurugram and Bengaluru. Finshots doesn't think this kills the gig delivery job, though. Drones can skip the traffic, but someone still has to carry the bag up the stairs.

A well-known study found that when cable TV reached rural Indian villages in the early 2000s, women's attitudes shifted. They reported more say at home, less tolerance of domestic violence and less preference for sons. Morning Brew · Sat Oct 3
US hiring slowed sharply. The economy added just 29,000 jobs in September, far below the 84,000 economists expected, and July and August were revised down by 60,000 combined. Unemployment ticked up to 4.2% and wage growth fell to 3%, its slowest since the pandemic. Oddly, stocks loved it, because weak hiring makes another Fed rate hike less likely.

ByteByteGo · Sat Oct 3
A short explainer on how SSH keeps a remote login safe. The two machines agree on encryption, the server proves who it is with its host key, and each side works out the same session key on its own so it never travels over the network. Then you log in by signing with a private key that never leaves your laptop.
