Netflix has started paying big YouTubers to post the exact same videos on both platforms on the same day, and YouTube is fighting back by offering those creators millions to stay exclusive. It sounds odd since YouTube is free and Netflix is paid, but YouTube now pulls about 13% of all US TV viewing against Netflix's 9%, and every minute someone watches a creator on Netflix instead is a minute YouTube can't sell ads against.
Finshots · Sun Aug 23
India throws out roughly 100 to 120 million milk pouches a day, and almost none get recycled because the multilayer plastic is such a pain to process. Mother Dairy spent four years on a pouch that naturally breaks down into a wax soil microbes can eat, without pushing up the price of milk. Separately, IISc Bengaluru figured out how to melt old pouches into a tough engineering plastic you can even 3D print with.

A clear side-by-side of the three ways people run open-weight models locally. Ollama queues requests one at a time and is really for laptops and prototyping. vLLM batches incoming requests into whatever is already running and squeezes the most out of a GPU, so it wins for heavy traffic. SGLang caches shared prompt prefixes, which makes it the pick for agents and long multi-turn chats where the same text keeps repeating.
ByteByteGo · Sat Aug 22
Anthropic wants to watermark the text its models write, and the trick is simpler than it sounds. Whenever the model has several equally good words to choose from, a secret key quietly narrows which ones it's allowed to pick. To check a piece of text later you just count how often the words line up with what the key would have allowed. Real AI text matches far more often than chance.
