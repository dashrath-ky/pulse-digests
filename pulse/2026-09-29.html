Finshots · Tue Sep 29
India wants to pull lithium, nickel and cobalt out of old batteries so it depends less on China, and it's offering ₹1,500 crore in subsidies to plants that do the actual extraction. The snag is "black mass," the dark powder left over after a battery is shredded. India banned exporting it, but shredders keep sneaking it out under the wrong labels because refiners in China and South Korea pay more and pay faster. That leaves Indian refiners like Rubamin and Attero short of the raw material they need.
