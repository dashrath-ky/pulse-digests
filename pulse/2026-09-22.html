Finshots · Tue Sep 22
India's rice harvest is heading for its worst drop since 2009. Output could fall about 6.5% to 144 million tonnes, because the monsoon came up 15% short nationally and as much as 42% short in big rice states like UP and Jharkhand. The government is sitting on roughly 60 million tonnes of stock, but a lot of that is unmilled paddy, reserved for ration shops, or being turned into ethanol, so it can't just be dumped on the market. Non-basmati prices are already up more than 10%, and pricier broken rice would push up egg, chicken and dairy costs too.

ByteByteGo · Mon Sep 21

An 8B model needs roughly 16GB just for weights at 16-bit, and your RAM and VRAM don't merge into one pool. The tricks that help: quantization, offloading layers as needed, mixture of experts, distillation, pruning, and speculative decoding.
Lenny's Newsletter · Mon Sep 21
A hands-on review of Meta's Muse argues it's the first personal AI agent that actually nails consumer UX. It asks for permission at the moment access becomes relevant rather than grabbing everything up front, shows a full activity feed of what it did, and treats open-ended things like sleep-training a baby as ongoing goals instead of one-shot tasks. It also avoids developer jargon entirely, no crons or plugins, just a feed, ideas, goals and a library. Browser shopping is still the weak spot: it fumbled a sneaker purchase and opened the wrong movie for tickets.
