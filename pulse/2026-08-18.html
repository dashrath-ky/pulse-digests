ByteByteGo · Mon Aug 17
A good side-by-side of how Waymo and Tesla actually build self-driving. Waymo measures the world with 13 cameras, four lidars, six radars and audio receivers out to 500m, then feeds it into a foundation model with a Gemini-based VLM for weird situations. Tesla uses cameras only and infers depth, which is cheaper but can be wrong. Waymo has 220.6 million driverless miles across five metros; Tesla has 30 billion miles a year but nearly all with a human responsible.
